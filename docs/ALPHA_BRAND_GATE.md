## Required before the next positioning change

1. Capture at least one verbatim customer or qualified-prospect observation with source, date, and touchpoint.
2. Compare that language with the desired identity and name one specific gap and cause.
3. Choose only one or two relevant perspectives: product, organization, person, or symbol.
4. State functional, emotional, and self-expressive value; validate rather than invent emotional language.
5. Change one highest-visibility surface first and record what must stop.
6. Define a 30-day metric, source, owner, and target.
7. Obtain human approval before publishing, outreach, pricing, or production changes.

## Stop rule

Do not rewrite the homepage, offer, proposal, or outreach angle from founder preference alone. If current-perception evidence is absent, keep the project in EVIDENCE_REQUIRED.
